Neon Voyage: a free pixel-art rocket game

Fly a neon rocket flat out through an endless asteroid field. Every near miss makes you faster and multiplies your points, so the best runs are the closest ones. Grab shields and boosts, and set off a nova when it gets crowded. It runs in any modern browser, on phones, tablets and computers, with nothing to install and no account.

Play now

How to play

Computer
The left and right arrow keys, or A and D. The rocket only moves side to side, fast, and leans into each turn. P or Esc pauses, M turns the sound off and on. After a crash, press any key to fly again.
Phone or tablet
Touch and hold the left or right side of the screen; slide across the middle to switch. Your thumbs stay at the edges, clear of the rocket. Tap to fly again after a crash. Add the page to your home screen to play full screen.
Gamepad
Left stick or d-pad left and right to steer, A to start, Start to pause.

Speed and scoring

  • Speed. The field speeds up the longer you last. Fly close to a rock without touching it (a graze) and you go faster still: each graze fills the speed gauge, and the gauge multiplies every point, up to ×5 at max speed. Stop grazing and the speed bleeds away.
  • Distance. Points for the distance you fly, so the faster you go, the faster you score.
  • Walls. Rows of rocks cross the lane with one gap to fly through. Later they come as slaloms, with the gaps on alternating sides.
  • Shards. Cyan crystals come in lines of five. Each is worth 25 points times your multiplier, and a whole line earns a bonus.
  • Smashing. Rocks broken by a shield, a boost or a nova score too. Asteroids knock each other about, so watch for one bouncing into your path, and every so often a meteor storm rolls in.

Made with Pixelbolt

Every sprite, effect and letter in Neon Voyage was made with Pixelbolt, an open-source pixel-art toolkit built for AI agents. Each piece of art is a small JSON recipe, and Pixelbolt turns it into exact pixels: no image model and no hand painting in an editor. An AI agent wrote these recipes, looked at what they drew and fixed them, the way Pixelbolt is meant to be used. The recipes are in the game's art folder, and art/build.mjs rebuilds every image from them.

The stars, nebulae and ringed planet use Pixelbolt's dithering and ramp shading, the score and messages are its built-in pixel font, and the image shown when you share a link is a Pixelbolt scene that places the same sprites. The game draws them all with Pixelbolt's atlas runtime. Try Pixelbolt in the browser playground or poke its live fluids.

Questions

Is Neon Voyage free?

Yes. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. The game is open source under the MIT licence.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in portrait or landscape, in any recent mobile browser. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. On iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen gives you a full-screen game.

Is my best score saved?

Your best score and sound settings stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Who made it?

Artem Skulimovskiy. The first version, drawn with plain canvas shapes, came out in 2024. This version was remade in November 2026 with Pixelbolt art, touch controls, working power-ups and sound.
